How it works

Every path starts with an application.

I read every application myself. It helps me understand where you are and what you want to change, and it makes sure the people I work with are ready to do the work.

  1. Apply. It takes about ten minutes.
  2. Hear back within a few days. If it looks like a good fit, I'll send you the next step.
  3. Circle applicants go on the waitlist for the founding cohort in March 2027. Nothing is charged until enrollment opens.
  4. Mentorship and Immersion applicants book a 60-minute Clarity Session with me over Zoom for $500. If you join within 14 days, the $500 goes toward your program. If you already know you are serious, you can book the Clarity Session now.

What you are investing in is more than twenty years of lived practice in land-based, ancestral and Indigenous knowledge. These are teachings held by elders and knowledge keepers and rarely shared in the open. I have spent my life earning them, practicing them and testing them against the research, and I have carried them into communities across the Pacific, the Americas, Europe, Australia and Southeast Asia. I have guided rites of passage for over a decade and led more than a thousand ceremonies. I am a custodian of land and sea. I live these teachings every day, and I only teach what I practice.

Before you begin

This work is an education and a practice. I share what I have learned and lived. Your part is discernment: take in what fits, test it in your own life, and keep asking questions. Change comes from what you put into practice, not from what you hear.

Nothing I teach replaces medical, mental health, legal or financial care from a licensed professional. You remain responsible for your own choices and your own health. What you share with me stays private, and calls are only recorded for your own use, with your permission.

Questions

Before you apply

Do I need to be a member of The Return?

For the Circle, yes. It builds on the daily practice of the membership, so you should have finished at least one month first. Mentorship and Immersion don't require it, and a year of membership is included in both.

I'm active in Sacred Sons. Can I join the Circle?

The Circle is for people who are not currently active in Sacred Sons. If you are active there now, keep putting your energy into that work. If you went to one event and want something more, or you are no longer active, you are welcome. Mentorship and Immersion are open to everyone.

Can I book a single session?

I no longer offer single sessions. The Clarity Session is the only one-off session, and it's for people who have applied for Mentorship or Immersion.

Is this only for men?

No. I work with women and men of all ages.

What happens if it isn't a fit?

I'll tell you honestly, and point you toward something that fits better, often The Return membership or the Circle.
