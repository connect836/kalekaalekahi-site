N.O.W. · New Old Way

Small-batch clothing, made only when it's ordered.

N.O.W. releases four limited drops a year. Each design is made in a run of 30 pieces or fewer and sold on pre-order for one week. When the week closes, that piece is gone.

How a drop works

One week to order, then it's made for you.

  1. Join the Drop List. You hear about each drop first, with the story behind the pieces.
  2. Get early access. The Drop List and members of The Return get the link 24 hours before everyone else.
  3. Order during the week. Each piece is capped at 30. Sizes go fast.
  4. It's made and shipped. Pieces are printed after the window closes and ship in about four weeks.

No restocks. A design can come back only as part of a new capsule.
